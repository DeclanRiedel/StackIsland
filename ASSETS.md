# Third-party assets

Every asset in this project is either made in code or CC0 (public domain). No attribution is legally required, but credit is nice.

| Pack | Author | Licence | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pirate Kit | Kenney | CC0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/pirate-kit | palms, barrels, flags, lighthouse, boats |
| Survival Kit | Kenney | CC0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/survival-kit | crates, campfires, tents, tools, workbenches |
| Nature Kit | Kenney | CC0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit | trees, rocks, crops, logs, decoration |
| Food Kit | Kenney | CC0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit | most product models |
| Mini Market | Kenney | CC0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-market | baskets, registers, freezers |
| Fantasy Town Kit 2.0 | Kenney | CC0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit | market stalls, windmills, watermills, carts |
| Cube Pets 1.0 | Kenney | CC0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/cube-pets | the crabs |
| Mini Characters | Kenney | CC0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters | the player, customers, helpers |

Each pack folder in `Assets/_Game/Art/ThirdParty/Kenney/<pack>/` keeps its original `License.txt`. Only the FBX models and their colour-map texture are included.

**Made in code (no files):** all sound effects (`Core/Sfx.cs`), meat, crab cakes, cash bundles, the dome grill, counter, walls, cage fences, round trees, floor signs and buy tiles (`Art/ProcArt.cs`), particle effects and UI sprites.
